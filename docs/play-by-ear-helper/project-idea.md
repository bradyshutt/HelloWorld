# Play by Ear Helper

## Overview

A system that listens to an audio clip of music you're trying to play (e.g., a song from the radio), uses AI to extract the pitches of specified instruments, and displays them as playable sheet music.

## Example Use Case

You hear something playing that you like, so you play it back and ask the system to listen and show you the notes.

## How It Works

1. **Audio Input** — The system listens to an audio clip of music.
2. **Instrument Selection** — The user specifies which instrument(s) to transcribe.
3. **AI Pitch Extraction** — AI analyzes the audio and extracts the pitches for the specified instruments.
4. **Sheet Music Output** — The extracted notes are displayed as playable sheet music.

## Goals

- Make it easy for musicians (and aspiring musicians) to learn songs by ear.
- Support multiple instruments per clip.
- Produce sheet music that is readable and playable.
